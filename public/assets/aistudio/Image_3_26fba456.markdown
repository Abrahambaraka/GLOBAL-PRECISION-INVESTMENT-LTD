## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-conviction, authoritative visual voice for an institutional investment conglomerate. Rooted in global industry leadership across modern mining, industrial supply infrastructure, and high-yield sustainable agriculture, the interface projects calculated risk management, systemic stability, and forward-looking technological execution.

The style merges **Corporate / Modern** discipline with **High-Contrast Precision**. It rejects decorative fluff, fleeting trend-chasing, and superficial transparency in favor of structural clarity, deep foundational navy tones, engineered metrics, and high-energy gold accents that signal capital growth and execution accuracy. The emotional goal is immediate trust, fiscal rigor, and forward momentum.

## Colors

The color palette centers on sovereign corporate stability, energetic performance, and enterprise-grade accessibility:

- **Primary (`#0f2c59`)**: Institutional deep navy anchoring top-level navigation, key structural headers, authoritative cards, and baseline typography.
- **Secondary (`#f9a825`)**: High-visibility dynamic gold serving strictly as the primary focal accelerator—driving high-impact CTAs, yield indicators, upward performance ticks, and executive action points.
- **Tertiary (`#1e4d8c`)**: Mid-tone precision cobalt used for interactive states, progress trajectories, secondary metrics, and contextual category badges (e.g., modern mining and logistics tags).
- **Neutral (`#475569`)**: Balanced slate gray ensuring high legibility across metadata, secondary metrics, data grids, and fine borders on crisp `#ffffff` canvas surfaces.
- **Surface Foundations**: Pure `#ffffff` primary background supported by neutral slate tint `#f8fafc` for card bodies and `#f1f5f9` for subtle divider structures.

## Typography

Typography pairs technical calculation with institutional neutrality. 

- **Space Grotesk** commands the headline hierarchy. Its structural, geometric glyphs deliver an engineered feel fitting for capital investments in mining machinery, supply chain routing, and tech-driven agriculture.
- **Inter** handles narrative copy, analytical tables, and micro-labels with neutral legibility, robust optical scaling, and clear numerical tabular alignment.
- Financial figures, metric rollups, and ticker deltas inherit strict tabular lining (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical wobble across live financial data feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 12-column responsive grid engineered for dense analytical visibility and institutional hierarchy:

- **Desktop (≥1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, with `gutter` at `1.5rem` and outer page `margin` of `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, adaptive margin at `2rem`, gutter maintained at `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, compact outer `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem`, and gutter at `1rem`.

Component spacing adheres to a strict 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-increment (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Information density stays tight in data grids and analytical cards to allow executive dashboards and asset performance overviews to be parsed without unnecessary scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast structural borders** combined with **subtle, cool-toned ambient shadows**:

- **Tier 0 (Baseline / Canvas)**: `#ffffff` for standard pages; `#0f2c59` deep canvas on corporate hero sections and key metric strips.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Cards)**: Pure white `#ffffff` elevated over `#f8fafc` background with a crisp border: `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover)**: Box shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Dropdowns & Modals)**: Box shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.06)`. Border: `1px solid #cbd5e1`.

No exaggerated blurs or skeuomorphic bevels are permitted. Every shadow is subtly tinted with the primary navy hue (`rgba(15, 44, 89, ...)`) to reinforce brand depth.

## Shapes

The system implements a **Soft (`1`)** shape geometry.

- Interactive elements (buttons, inputs, status badges) use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, data containers, and modal dialogs scale up to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Pill shapes are reserved exclusively for contextual status chips (e.g., "Active", "Yielding", "Matured").

This restraint reflects institutional seriousness, avoiding the overly playful feel of heavy pill shapes while preventing the harshness of raw 0px corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#f9a825` gold fill with `#0f2c59` navy bold text. Hover state shifts to `#fbc02d` with a crisp `0 2px 8px rgba(249, 168, 37, 0.35)` elevation. Active state shifts to `#f57f17`. Radius: `0.25rem`.
- **Institutional Primary**: Deep navy `#0f2c59` fill with pure `#ffffff` text. Hover shifts to tertiary `#1e4d8c`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Transparent fill, `1.5px solid #0f2c59` border, navy text. Hover applies `#0f2c59` at 5% opacity tint.
- **Ghost**: Zero border, text color `#0f2c59`, hover background `#f1f5f9`.

### Corporate Cards
- **Portfolio & Asset Card**: Crisp white surface, `1px solid #e2e8f0` outline, 8px padding radius. Features an accent top-border rule (`3px`) tinted according to portfolio division: gold for modern mining, deep navy for logistics, emerald/slate for sustainable agriculture.
- **Stat Summary Card**: Displays large `Space Grotesk` metrics (32px), subtle uppercase Inter metadata label (11px, slate `#64748b`), and directional indicator icons (green/gold/slate).

### Chips & Badges
- Minimal height (24px - 28px), uppercase or capitalized Inter label (11px).
- Division Tags: Soft neutral tinted backgrounds with high-contrast text (`#0f2c59` on `#e2e8f0`, or `#f9a825` on `#fef9c3`). Radius: `9999px` (pill) for status badges, `0.25rem` for taxonomy tags.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Height: 42px standard. Border: `1px solid #cbd5e1`. Background: `#ffffff`.
- Focus state: `1.5px solid #0f2c59` border with a crisp focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.12)`.
- Helper text in 12px Inter `#64748b`. Error states shift border to `#dc2626`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox: 18x18px, `0.25rem` radius, `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`. Checked: filled `#0f2c59` with crisp `#ffffff` checkmark.
- Radio: 18x18px circular, checked state has `#0f2c59` outer circle with an inner `#ffffff` gap and solid `#0f2c59` central bullseye.

### Data Tables & Asset Breakdown Lists
- Row lines use `1px solid #f1f5f9`. Header row styled in `#f8fafc` with 11px uppercase slate `#64748b` text and strict letter spacing (`0.05em`).
- Numeric cell contents right-aligned with tabular font numerals. Alternating hover background `#f8fafc`.
